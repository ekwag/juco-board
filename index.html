What this is not

Production only. There is no video here, no defensive evaluation, no velocity, no body or makeup read, and no idea who else is recruiting these players. A high number means he hit; it does not mean he can play for you. This is a list of who to go look at, not a list of who to take.

Built from published season statistics for 2026, across the loaded states, filtered to freshmen with 60+ plate appearances or 20.0+ innings. Score is a percentile blend within this population, computed separately for wood-bat schools — for hitters production (50%), strikeout rate (30%), isolated power (20%); for pitchers strikeouts (35%), ERA (30%), WHIP (20%), walk rate (15%). It ranks who to look at first; it does not evaluate anyone. Notes save to the shared board and every signed-in reviewer sees them. Export writes them out as JSON.
